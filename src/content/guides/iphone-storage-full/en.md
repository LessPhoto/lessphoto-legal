## Start by finding the actual limit

Device storage and iCloud storage are separate. Open Settings > General > iPhone Storage to inspect the phone, then Settings > your name > iCloud to inspect cloud usage. Fix the one that is actually full.

- Device full: apps may fail to update, the camera may stop saving, and the phone has less working room.
- iCloud full: backups and photo syncing may pause even when the phone still has free space.

## Use the least expensive reversible step first

Review large videos, screenshots, downloads, exact duplicates, and photos you already know you do not need. If the library still matters but local space is tight, iCloud Photos with Optimize Storage can reduce local use without removing the library from view.

> Do not delete photos just to remove their local copy while iCloud Photos is on. A deletion syncs to every device using that library.

## When paying is reasonable

iCloud+ is useful when you want syncing, backup capacity, and access across devices. More built-in iPhone storage is useful when you need originals, large apps, or offline video available without a connection. Many people benefit from both; they solve different constraints.
