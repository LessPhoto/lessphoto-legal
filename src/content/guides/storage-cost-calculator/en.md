## What the result means

The calculator compares cash cost only. Local storage is fast, offline capacity tied to one phone. iCloud is recurring cloud capacity used for syncing, backups, files, and shared access. A lower number does not automatically make one choice better.

## A useful way to decide

Use the time horizon you normally keep a phone. Enter the real storage-upgrade price difference shown when you buy, then compare it with the iCloud plan you would actually need. Finally, consider whether cleanup or compression could delay either purchase.

> iCloud+ prices are regional and can change. The calculator labels when its presets were checked and lets you edit the device upgrade cost.
