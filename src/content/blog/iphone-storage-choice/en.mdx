## New iPhones are getting more expensive

The iPhone upgrade season has arrived again, and careful buyers may notice that higher-storage models cost quite a bit more this year. One reason is the rising price of storage chips: a 2TB iPhone 18 Pro Max is now close to $3,280.

With storage getting more expensive, should you buy more built-in storage or a smaller iPhone paired with iCloud?

## What a storage upgrade really costs

Here, “memory” means the phone’s storage capacity. The comparison starts with the 256GB model and uses the iPhone 18 Pro Max price ladder as a reference. USD figures are approximate conversions for comparison, and actual prices vary by market.

Going from 256GB to 512GB, 1TB, or 2TB costs about $298, $820, or $1,565 more upfront. After three years, the extra storage usually does not retain the full price difference when you sell the phone. For a rough estimate, this comparison assumes that only 20% of the extra capacity premium remains in resale value:

| Upgrade | Upfront premium | Estimated resale premium after 3 years | Actual 3-year extra | Average per month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 256GB → 512GB | $298 | $60 | $238 | about $7 |
| 256GB → 1TB | $820 | $164 | $656 | about $18 |
| 256GB → 2TB | $1,565 | $313 | $1,252 | about $35 |

## A 256GB iPhone plus iCloud costs less

If photos and videos are what fill your phone, a smaller iPhone paired with iCloud may be more economical. This table compares only the extra storage cost over three years:

| iPhone upgrade | Actual 3-year hardware extra | iCloud+ reference plan | Three-year iCloud cost |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 512GB | $238 | 200GB | $113 |
| 1TB | $656 | 2TB shared by two people | about $182 per person |
| 2TB | $1,252 | 2TB for one person | $365 |

iCloud does not offer a 1TB plan, so the 1TB comparison uses a two-person 2TB family plan. Over three years, the iCloud options cost less than paying for the equivalent storage upgrade. A 256GB iPhone paired with iCloud is the more economical choice under these assumptions.

If you regularly play large mobile games, however, more local storage can still be worth it.

## If you use iCloud, keep this in mind

After turning on iCloud Photos, also turn on **Optimize iPhone Storage**. The iPhone keeps thumbnail versions locally while full-resolution originals stay in iCloud.

Also, iCloud Photos is a sync feature, not an independent backup. If you delete a photo on the iPhone, it is also deleted from iCloud and other devices using the same library.

## Clean up regularly to control your iCloud bill

iCloud can store photos and videos, but the library will keep growing. The price also rises with the plan: 6TB costs about $1,062 over three years, while 12TB costs about $2,136. Whether you choose a larger iPhone or iCloud, regular cleanup can delay the next upgrade.

iPhone’s built-in cleanup tools are limited when it comes to similar bursts. LessPhoto can group similar photos for comparison and batch deletion.

Sharing a 2TB plan between two people and cleaning up regularly can keep the storage budget under control.
